Payments

Configure how customers can pay you directly, and see a log of payment attempts.

UPI (GPay, PhonePe, Paytm, BHIM)

UPI is interoperable — one UPI ID works from any UPI app. Customers scan a QR code or tap a "Pay with GPay/PhonePe" button on the site; the payment happens directly in their bank app, with no gateway fees.

Card & Netbanking (Razorpay)

For cards, netbanking, and wallets, you'll need a free Razorpay account — sign up at razorpay.com, complete their KYC, then paste your API keys below (Settings → API Keys in your Razorpay dashboard).

Transaction Log

UPI payments are logged when a customer says they've paid — verify against your actual bank/UPI app before marking them paid. Razorpay payments are verified automatically.
