Who We Are

Built by people who
actually drive these roads.

Touring Buddiez started in Guwahati with one idea: Northeast India rewards travelers who go with people who know it — not a fixed brochure route. Every itinerary is built around your dates, your pace and the season's actual road and weather conditions.

From the living root bridges of Meghalaya to Bhutan's monastery passes, our driver‑guides have covered the ground themselves — so you get the waterfall that's actually running, and skip the one that isn't.

  • Local driver-guides, not outsourced vendors
  • Itineraries built around your dates, not a template
  • Transparent quotes — no hidden add-ons
