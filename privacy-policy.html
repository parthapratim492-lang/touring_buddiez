Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

Touring Buddiez ("we", "us", "our") operates touringbuddieznortheast.in. This page explains what information we collect when you use this site or enquire about a trip, and how we use it.

Information we collect

When you submit a booking, enquiry, or review form on this site, we collect the details you provide directly — typically your name, phone number, email address, travel dates, group size, and any message you include. We don't ask for payment details through this website.

How we use it

  • To respond to your enquiry or booking request, by phone, WhatsApp, or email.
  • To plan and coordinate the trip or rental you've asked about.
  • To display an approved review publicly if you submit one through our review form (only your name, rating, and message are shown — your email and phone number are never published).

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties.

WhatsApp & third-party communication

Several links on this site open WhatsApp to start a conversation with us directly. Messages sent that way are subject to WhatsApp's own privacy policy, not this one.

Cookies & analytics

This site may use basic, privacy-respecting analytics to understand overall traffic patterns (for example, which pages are visited most). This data is aggregated and isn't used to individually identify you.

Data retention

We retain booking and enquiry information for as long as reasonably necessary to fulfil your request and for basic record-keeping. You can ask us to delete your information at any time — see contact details below.

Your rights

You can ask us what information we hold about you, request a correction, or request deletion, at any time by contacting us directly.
